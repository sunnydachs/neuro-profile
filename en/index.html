About this check

Across 5 cognitive axes (motivation and direction, processing style, processing target, interpersonal orientation, and emotional stability), we infer which of the 16 types your answer pattern is closest to.

  • Time: ~8–12 minutes
  • Items: 50 (40 scoring + 10 reliability checks)
  • Answer style: 5-point Likert scale

This is not an fMRI or EEG brain measurement. It is a self-check that infers your cognitive tendencies from your answers. It does not make a medical diagnosis or judge ability — please treat the result as one reference for self-understanding.
